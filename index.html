How We Work

From Brief to Build in 5 Steps

A clear, transparent process so you always know where your project stands.

1

Brief & Consultation

Tell us your fair dates, floor space, budget, and brand objectives. We listen and ask the right questions.

2

Concept Design

Our designers produce 3D renders and floor plans. You review, feedback, and approve.

3

Production

All components manufactured in our Belgrade workshop with strict quality control at every stage.

4

Transport & Install

Our team handles delivery and on-site assembly. Your stand is ready before the fair opens.

Dismantle & Store

After the fair, we dismantle, pack, and store your stand — ready for the next event.
